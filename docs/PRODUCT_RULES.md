# Product Rules

## Purpose

This document defines the non-negotiable product boundaries of Momenta.

The goal is to preserve product identity as the application grows.

Features may evolve.

Technology may evolve.

Design may evolve.

Product identity must remain stable.

---

# Product Definition

Momenta is:

A personal life timeline companion.

Momenta helps users:

* remember important things
* understand time passing
* prepare for upcoming events
* maintain awareness of life moments

Momenta is not a task manager.

Momenta is not a habit tracker.

Momenta is not a calendar replacement.

Momenta is not a social platform.

Momenta is not an AI chatbot.

---

# Core Promise

Remember What Matters.

Everything in Momenta must reinforce this promise.

---

# Product Pillars

## Pillar 1

Time Awareness

Users understand:

* how long ago
* how long until
* what is approaching

---

## Pillar 2

Important Moments

Momenta focuses on meaningful life items.

Not micro tasks.

---

## Pillar 3

Calm Experience

The product should reduce stress.

Never create stress.

---

## Pillar 4

Privacy

Private life data remains private.

---

## Pillar 5

Widget-first Value

Widgets are a primary experience.

Not a secondary feature.

---

# Accepted Use Cases

Examples:

Vehicle inspection

Vehicle maintenance

Doctor appointments

Dentist visits

Insurance renewals

Pet vaccinations

Family reminders

Important anniversaries

Personal milestones

Life events

Home maintenance

Health checkups

---

# Rejected Use Cases

Momenta should not optimize for:

shopping lists

daily tasks

project management

team collaboration

issue tracking

CRM workflows

employee management

inventory management

school management

workplace productivity systems

---

# Todo Boundary

Users may create moments that resemble tasks.

Example:

Call Mom

This is acceptable.

However:

Momenta must never become a task management system.

Forbidden:

* task hierarchies
* subtask trees
* project boards
* kanban systems
* sprint systems

---

# Habit Tracker Boundary

Momenta may reference recurring life events.

However:

Momenta is not a habit tracker.

Forbidden:

* streak obsession
* streak loss anxiety
* gamified habit loops
* daily completion pressure

---

# Social Boundary

Momenta is personal.

Forbidden:

* public feeds
* likes
* followers
* comments
* social rankings

---

# Productivity Boundary

Momenta is awareness-first.

Not productivity-first.

The goal is:

awareness

not optimization.

---

# AI Boundary

AI is not the product.

If AI is introduced in the future:

AI supports Momenta.

Momenta does not revolve around AI.

Forbidden:

AI chat replacing the core product.

---

# Notification Rules

Notifications must:

* inform
* remind
* support

Notifications must not:

* guilt
* pressure
* manipulate

---

# Insight Rules

Insights should:

* increase awareness
* provide context
* encourage action

Insights should not:

* judge
* shame
* diagnose

---

# Premium Rules

Premium must unlock value.

Premium must not create frustration.

Avoid:

artificial pain

forced upgrades

dark patterns

---

# Widget Rules

Widgets must provide value without opening the app.

Widgets are not advertisements.

Widgets are not upsell surfaces.

---

# Home Screen Rule

Home must answer:

What matters right now?

within seconds.

---

# Simplicity Rule

Before adding a feature ask:

Does this help users remember what matters?

If not:

the feature likely does not belong.

---

# Feature Acceptance Checklist

A feature should generally satisfy:

1. supports time awareness
2. supports meaningful moments
3. respects privacy
4. supports widget-first experience
5. fits calm product philosophy
6. works offline
7. avoids complexity

---

# Feature Rejection Checklist

Reject if the feature:

1. creates productivity bloat
2. creates social complexity
3. creates task-management complexity
4. duplicates another app category
5. weakens Momenta's identity
6. exists only because competitors have it

---

# Product Evolution Rule

Momenta may expand.

But expansion must strengthen:

time awareness

not dilute it.

---

# Final Product Question

Before implementing any feature ask:

Would removing this feature make Momenta less capable of helping users remember what matters?

If the answer is no,

the feature should probably not be built.

---

# Final Rule

Momenta is a life timeline companion.

Everything else is secondary.
