# Post MVP Roadmap

## Purpose

This document defines Momenta's long-term evolution after MVP.

The goal is:

* controlled growth
* feature prioritization
* product identity preservation
* roadmap clarity

Growth should strengthen Momenta.

Growth should never dilute Momenta.

---

# Roadmap Philosophy

Priority order:

Reliability

↓

Retention

↓

Expansion

↓

Monetization

↓

Advanced Features

---

Never:

Feature Count

↓

Quality

---

---

# Roadmap Evaluation Rule

Before adding a roadmap item ask:

Does this strengthen time awareness?

If not:

it probably does not belong.

---

# Product Identity Check

Every roadmap phase must preserve:

* calm experience
* privacy-first
* widget-first
* offline-first
* life timeline focus

---

# Final Rule

The roadmap exists to guide growth.

Not every possible feature deserves to be built.
